# Safety signage — important notice

The safety-sign hotspots in EVACTIS 3DWORKFLOW (emergency exit, first aid, AED,
assembly point, fire extinguisher, fire alarm, warning) follow the **colour and shape
conventions of ISO 7010** (green square = safe condition, red square = fire equipment,
yellow triangle with black border = warning). The pictograms are Evactis drawings made
to match the Evactis button kit; they are **not** reproductions of ISO artwork.

**Signage used in a real deliverable must be reviewed by a qualified person against the
codes and standards that apply to the facility** (for example the National Building
Code of Canada, provincial fire codes, CSA standards and the authority having
jurisdiction). The software does not claim, and its UI must never state, compliance
with ISO 7010 or any code.

The same applies to evacuation routes and equipment locations: they are always placed
or confirmed by a person (the schema records who and when), and every printed plan
carries a "verify on site" note and a date.
